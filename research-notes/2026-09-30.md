- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37976

Motivated by this finding, we propose Spectral Null-Space Swap ($S^3$), a training-free composition of paired Non-thinking and Thinking checkpoints. ### A Dominant Supplier Slows Recursive Drift More Than It Steers It

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-09-30
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11146
- Matched layers: market_context

If one supplier writes most of a shared corpus, does it pull the models trained on it toward its own writing, or change how fast they drift? The departure leans toward Phi-2 more as its share grows, but more than toward every other model only at 90%.
